# Work Shift & Wage Calculator (iOS PWA) — v3.5.0

A standalone shift tracker and wage calculator web app designed with native iOS Human Interface aesthetics (Light & Dark Mode). It enables daily shift logging on an interactive visual calendar, supports multi-language setups, and calculates total hours and earnings in real time — 100% offline.

### 🚀 Key Features:
- **Native iOS Aesthetic (Dual Mode)**: Clean visual interface featuring squircle date tiles, a 4-column summary grid, and seamless switching between **Light Mode** and **Dark Mode**.
- **Multi-Language Support (3 Languages)**: Built-in switcher supporting **Türkçe (TR)**, **Bahasa Indonesia (ID)**, and **English (EN)** with localized weekdays, months, and UI labels.
- **Dynamic Month & Year Navigation**: Browse forward or backward across any month and year with independent monthly schedule storage.
- **One-Tap PDF Export & Print Sheet**: Clean, paper-optimized A4 printable view that automatically switches to a high-contrast light layout during export.
- **Interactive Shift System**:
  - ⚪ **White**: Standard shift (default: 9 hours)
  - 🟡 **Yellow**: Mid shift (default: 7 hours)
  - 🔴 **Red**: Day off (0 hours)
  - 🔵 **Custom**: Flexible shift (default: 3 hours or customizable)
- **Configurable Durations & Rates**: In-app settings sheet to adjust default durations for each shift category as well as the hourly wage rate (default: 130 TL).
- **Instant Automatic Calculations**: Total working hours and estimated wages update immediately on the sticky bottom bar.
- **100% Offline & Zero-Dependency PWA**: Completely self-contained with no external CDNs or network calls. Fully functional via Safari's *Add to Home Screen* with persistent local caching and Service Worker support.
